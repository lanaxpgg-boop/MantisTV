# Physical compatibility — exact tested target

**Partial functional compatibility verified for the unchanged 0.4.0-preview APK on one AFTMM/mantis, Fire OS 6.7.1.1(NS6711/5908), Android7.1.2/API 25, armeabi-v7a.** Installation, Home, remote navigation, app launch/return, search, local watchlist/profile1↔2 isolation, process persistence, screensaver and Amazon Home escape have linked evidence in DEVICE-TEST-REPORT.md. Clock defects and unverified controls remain. This is not full product acceptance.

Actual SDK25 execution and 32-bit device identity supplement prior compilation/DEX-only packaging evidence. No firmware update/downgrade, media/DRM/codec/HDMI/CEC validation, reboot, app update/removal or full-system replacement was tested. Original Amazon HOME retained; no package removed or disabled.

The firmware catalog's 41full/35incremental source records remain historical C labels until reconciled with this exact build identifier. This report supplies exact-build physical evidence; do not promote every NS6711 variant, every incremental edge or the entire family to A. A family-wide support requirement remains mandatory but unverified. Future capability probes must check API/service/permission/intent results per build and provide functional fallbacks, without hiding unsupported controls as working features.
